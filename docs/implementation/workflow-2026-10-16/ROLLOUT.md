activation, rehearse backup/restore in isolation against representative retained
state and prove that accepted newer history survives. Preserve previous compatible
reader/export/config identities. An older reader that cannot parse the new state
is not a valid rollback; use a compatible reader or explicit owned recovery.

updates, crash around CI dispatch, delayed native visibility, duplicate/out-of-order
delivery and service rollout failure. Hold uncertain reservations until actual
reconciliation proves the outcome. Never restart the retired minute timer beside
the event service. No destructive restore, live consumer deployment or paid CI
demonstration is required merely to test this plan.
